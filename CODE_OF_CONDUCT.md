# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This is a community resource. We want contributing to it to be a respectful,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, or
which company they work for — including companies that compete with the maintainer.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Keeping entries and discussion **factual and neutral** (the same standard the list
  itself holds).
- Welcoming additions from anyone, including competitors of the maintainer.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Assuming good faith.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Using pull requests or issues purely to disparage a competitor or promote a product
  with marketing copy.
- Bad-faith edits intended to bias the list for or against any vendor.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — issues, pull requests, and any other
communication tied to this repository.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, decline, or remove contributions that violate this code, and may
block accounts for repeated or egregious violations. To report a concern, open an issue
(use the `neutrality:` prefix for fairness concerns) or contact the maintainers privately.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/),
version 2.1.
